Mnemos

Mnemos watches where you stand, which way you circle and how you dash. Rely on a habit and it learns to punish it. Its memory carries over between rounds, so change how you play.

Move with WASD or arrow keys, or drag inside the arena. Dash with Space. You fire at the boss automatically. P pauses.
